### Vertical 1 — Specialty Healthcare
**Why:** Existing clients (Alliance Homecare, Trusthouse, Emerald Health). Dan: "HIPAA compliance, high capital and labor intensity. Opportunities arise from AI-driven consolidation and automation. Healthcare operators tend to be more educated and open to tech investment."
**Subverticals:** Home Care (skilled + non-skilled), Behavioral Health (outpatient, IOP/PHP), Digital Health, Specialty practices (orthopedic, cardiology, oncology billing)
**Pain points:** HIPAA-compliant data flows, intake-to-billing leakage, manual referral coordination, no real-time census/utilization, multi-location reporting in spreadsheets, paying for HubSpot/Salesforce that nobody uses
**Value angle:** "Stop billing leakage and prove ROI on every tech dollar — without hiring a $400K healthcare CTO."
**Signals:** hiring CTO/VP IT/RevOps; recent EMR or PMS purchase without integration; multi-location growth (1→3+); PE/HCIT investment; HIPAA breach disclosure; new behavioral-health facility opening

### Vertical 2 — Specialty Veterinary
**Why:** Dan: "Veterinary specialty centers are large and under-optimized compared to consolidated general practice market dominated by PE." PE consolidated general practice; specialty (surgical, ER, internal medicine) is fragmented and tech-naive — green field.
**Subverticals:** Specialty surgical centers, Veterinary ER networks, Multi-disciplinary referral hospitals, Veterinary behavioral
**Pain points:** Manual scheduling between primary and specialty referrals, no client portal, paper records or unintegrated PIMS, no marketing attribution, multi-location P&L impossible to consolidate
**Value angle:** "Consolidate 3 hospital P&Ls, automate referral intake, eliminate the spreadsheet your office manager built — in 8 weeks."
**Signals:** opening 2nd or 3rd location, recently hired hospital administrator, PIMS migration job posting, AVMA specialty accreditation added, recent PE/buyout activity

### Vertical 3 — Consumer Services & Premium Multi-Location
**Why:** Existing clients (Pups Pet Club, Calendar Group, Biscuits & Bath). Multi-location service businesses break every manual process when scaling 1→3+ locations. Tech-naive but cash-flow-positive.
**Subverticals:** Premium pet services (boarding, grooming networks), Calendar/concierge services, Boutique fitness, Med spas, Premium home services
**Pain points:** Each location runs differently, no centralized booking/CRM, owner is bottleneck on every report, can't see which location is profitable, can't scale marketing because attribution is absent
**Value angle:** "Stop being the only person who knows what's happening across all 3 locations — get a single dashboard in 4 weeks."
**Signals:** opening new location, hiring multi-unit GM, franchise expansion, multi-state licensing, recent SBA loan or capital event

### Vertical 4 — Specialty HR Staffing & Recruiting
**Why:** Less prioritized in calls but RevOps fit is strong. Recruiting firms live and die by their ATS/CRM stitching.
**Subverticals:** Healthcare staffing, Executive search, Specialty/technical recruiting, Light industrial staffing
**Pain points:** ATS doesn't talk to CRM doesn't talk to billing, candidate-to-placement reporting in spreadsheets, no source-of-hire attribution, manual hours/billing for contract placements
**Value angle:** "Stop double-keying candidates between ATS and HubSpot — fix the integration in 4 weeks."
**Signals:** recent ATS/CRM purchase, new VP Sales hire, branch expansion, MSP contract win, capacity scaling job postings

### Vertical 5 — Financial Services (Payment Solutions / Specialty Finance)
**Why:** Joey's strongest existing pipeline conversations. Payment processors, specialty lenders, embedded-finance plays need RevOps + AI agents for underwriting and customer service automation.
**Subverticals:** Payment processors / ISOs, Specialty lenders, Embedded finance platforms, Insurance brokerages (specialty)
**Pain points:** Application-to-funding takes too long, manual underwriting, no customer service automation, compliance reporting in spreadsheets, no real-time portfolio visibility
**Value angle:** "Cut application-to-decision time in half with an AI underwriting copilot — without replacing your core platform."
**Signals:** new compliance hire, ISO partnership announcements, Series A/B raise, RegTech vendor evaluations, hiring AI/ML engineers

**Negative signals (skip):** Single location AND <$5M revenue · Founder/CEO has CTO background · Recently bought competing product · Tech-native company with strong eng team · Pre-revenue / pre-seed · Consulting/agency shop.

**Buying cycle timing:**
- Soft offer ($6.5K/mo Advisory) — 30-day cycle. Best for post-funding founders with board pressure.
- Embedded / RevOps Build — 60-day cycle. Best for COOs with operational pain + budget.
- Transformation ($22.5K/mo) — 90-day cycle. Best for post-Series-B or post-PE with transformation mandate.
