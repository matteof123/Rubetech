# RubeTech Partners — Email & LinkedIn Sequence Templates

> Last updated: 2026-05-12
> Status: Pre-launch templates. To be finalized + tested with first 50 sends per vertical starting May 20.

These are template starting points per vertical. Each campaign run will personalize per-lead using Gemini Flash, but the structure, hook, proof point, and CTA below are the canonical references.

## Rules (apply to all templates)

- 50–80 words per email
- No em dashes
- No exclamation marks
- No spam words (no "free," "guaranteed," "cost," "$" in body — exception: PS or value props with care)
- Max 3-word subject lines, lowercase except company / person names
- Never use bracket placeholders like `[first_name]` — copy IS the final rendered text
- Every variable must be non-empty (including PS line)
- Never include "Best," sender name, or title in body — EmailBison `{SENDER_EMAIL_SIGNATURE}` handles signature
- Email 1 = Lead Magnet (Free 15-Minute Revenue Leak Audit)
- Email 2 = Service Offer (Fractional CTO or RevOps Build) — new thread, new subject line
- Email 3 = breakup, new thread, gentle

## LinkedIn Sequences (per Miro Process Flow)

**Message 1 — Signal-triggered, short, specific:**
- Reference the trigger directly (opening location #3, recent Series A, new VP Ops hire)
- Reference vertical proof point (Alliance Homecare, Pups Pet Club, CorCon USA, Trusthouse, Calendar Group)
- Personal angle (operator-to-operator, not consultant)
- Question only — NO pitch in first message
- Stay under 300 characters (LinkedIn cutoff)

**Message 2 — Asset share:**
- Choose one based on vertical:
  - Healthcare AI Industry Report (already produced)
  - Fractional CTO ROI calculator
  - "From spreadsheets to systems" one-pager (CorCon USA narrative)
