## Company Overview

**RubeTech Partners** is a boutique AI and technology consulting firm headquartered in the US Northeast (Connecticut) with Chicago/Midwest representation. Launched August 2025 by **Dan Rubenstein** (Managing Partner — 27y operator, 4x founder, 8-figure PE exit, ex-Accenture) and **Joseph Rios** (Partner — outbound, marketing, qualification).

They sit between fractional advisory firms and HubSpot implementation agencies, but unlike either, they **advise AND build** — taking a client from strategic roadmap to working revenue infrastructure end-to-end. Their differentiation: every engagement is led by people who have personally been the executive making multi-million-dollar tech decisions AND can write the code themselves.

Revenue trajectory: ~$50–75K last year (Aug 2025 launch) → ~$300K current pace → $1.5M ARR target in 12 months. Avg deal $100K/yr. 25% conversion on first meetings. Goal: 10 new clients in 12 months.

  - Day-to-day: Owns P&L, manages 3+ branch managers, fields every escalation. Top metrics: census/utilization, hours billed vs scheduled, caregiver retention, payor mix
  - Biggest frustrations: Can't see which branch is profitable in real time, billing leakage from missed visits, manual referral coordination with hospitals, can't forecast Medicaid/Medicare reimbursement

  - Day-to-day: Often clinician-owner. Mornings in surgery, afternoons in admin. Inherits IT decisions made by office manager. Tools: PIMS (Cornerstone, AVImark, ezyVet) + QuickBooks + fragmented marketing
  - Biggest frustrations: Referral hospitals don't get clean handoffs, client comms is manual, no marketing attribution, multi-location P&L impossible without office manager working overtime
  - Buying role: Shared with hospital administrator if one exists
  - ✅ Target when: 2+ locations OR $10M+ rev, recently hired hospital administrator, PIMS migration in last 6 months
  - ❌ Skip when: Single-doctor practice, general practice (PE-consolidated)

- **Multi-Location Owner-Op (Consumer Services V3)** — Founder / CEO, Multi-Unit Services; $3–25M
  - Day-to-day: Founder still in every meeting. Mondays pulling location reports, Fridays approving payroll. Tools: location-specific POS + QuickBooks + Excel. CRM exists but nobody uses it
  - Biggest frustrations: Can't compare locations side-by-side, doesn't know which marketing channel drives revenue, every new location resets the process
  - Buying role: Founder = budget. Decision in 2–3 calls if ROI is concrete
  - ✅ Target when: 3+ locations, founder still operating, recent expansion/SBA loan/franchise sale
  - ❌ Skip when: Single location, owner-only operation, franchise of large brand (corporate handles tech)

- **ISO/Payments Founder (FinServ V5)** — Founder / CEO, Payment Processor or ISO; $5–80M
  - Day-to-day: Heads sales + risk + tech, often half-tech founder. Recently raised, board pushing for AI strategy. Tools: core processor + Salesforce + Snowflake + ad hoc Python
  - Biggest frustrations: Board demanding AI roadmap, internal team can't agree on vendor stack, hiring a $400K CTO for 12 months they don't need feels wrong
  - Buying role: CEO + CTO + board. Decision driven by board mandate
  - ✅ Target when: Series A/B closed in last 12 mo, no CTO or recent CTO departure, AI/ML hiring posts
  - ❌ Skip when: Seed-stage, deeply tech-led founders, large incumbent processor
