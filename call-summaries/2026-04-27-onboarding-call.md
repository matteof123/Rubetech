## Key Discussion Points

- **Core vs auxiliary offer decision:** Fractional CTO is core. RevOps Build is auxiliary.
  - Reason: Fractional CTO creates deeper integration and recurring revenue ($6.5K–$22.5K/mo with 6–12 month minimums). RevOps Build is one-time work and smaller in scope.
  - Strategic implication: outbound should lead with Fractional CTO messaging for $10M+ companies, and RevOps Build messaging only for $1M–$10M companies where Fractional CTO is not yet affordable.

- **Vertical focus:** Healthcare + Specialty Veterinary as primary. Multi-Location Consumer Services + FinServ + HR Staffing as secondary.

- **Geography:** Northeast US (CT, NY, NJ, MA, PA) for Fractional CTO — Dan can deliver in-person. National for RevOps Build (remote-deliverable).

- **Buyer profile:** CEO / COO / Founder with budget authority. For RevOps: VP Sales / RevOps Director / Chief of Staff as champions.

- **Triggers prioritized:**
  - Absence of strategic technology leadership (no CTO in seat)
  - Multi-location expansion
  - Recent capital event (Series A/B in last 12 mo)
  - New C-suite hire (CEO/COO/CFO in last 6 mo)
  - Recent CRM purchase (HubSpot, Salesforce) without implementation

- **Lead magnet alternatives:** The 15-min Revenue Leak Audit is currently primary. Discovery → Brief (60-min discovery + written ROI brief) being tested as alternative for enterprise prospects who won't engage with a 15-min audit. Both should be tested in Wave 1.
